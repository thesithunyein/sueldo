# Code of conduct

Sueldo is a small project. The people who use it, file issues, or send changes should be able to do that without being insulted, harassed, or shut out.

## Expected

- Talk about the product and the problem, not the person.
- Assume a good reason for a question. Many owners are not developers.
- Do not post private keys, staff names from a real shop, or wage data that is not yours to share.
- Do not use this project to move money you are not allowed to move.

## Not accepted

Insults, threats, sexual remarks aimed at a person, and sharing someone's private information.

## Report it

Email [sithunyein.mailto@gmail.com](mailto:sithunyein.mailto@gmail.com). Say what happened and where. The maintainer, Sithu Nyein, will reply and may remove comments, block an account, or reject a change.
