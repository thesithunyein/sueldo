FAQ

Short answers.

Do I need a wallet?

No. Leave it empty and hand them the pesos. The page only writes the week.

What does closing the week do?

It marks the ready lines as paid. It sends money only if a pay account is set up.

Why is someone not paid?

The reason is on their line. Over a limit, too many hours, or already paid this week.
